Quick Reference Guide

Core Terminology

  • Playbook: Master runbook or SOP project container.
  • Sequence: Linear flow of procedural steps for execution.
  • Steps: Individual instruction unit displayed on the canvas.
  • Screen: Structural decision points between sequences.
